Since 2000

A Brazilian company that chose the United States to work and live.

Founded in 2000 by Edvaldo Leão, the bakery started in Deerfield Beach with one focus: bread and cakes made with care. Today it serves South Florida from two counters, Deerfield Beach and Coconut Creek.
